Downloads continue when Firefox closes. The companion app exits after five idle minutes. To update yt-dlp, finish or cancel your downloads and re-run your platform installer. On Windows, use a newly built host bundle.

YouTube needs Deno (recommended) or Node.js 22+ on PATH. Linux: sudo pacman -S ffmpeg deno. Windows: winget install DenoLand.Deno and winget install Gyan.FFmpeg. Restart the companion app after installing tools.

Private diagnostics

Only bounded operational codes and dependency versions are kept locally. Capture stops after 15 minutes, 1 MiB, or companion exit. No URLs, titles, paths, filenames, cookies, or tokens are captured. Nothing is uploaded.

Older raw logs may contain download activity. Choose whether to keep or remove them.
